About SkyWatch TSU

SkyWatch TSU is a dynamic weather data platform designed to provide accurate and up-to-date weather information at your fingertips. Built by the talented GDG team at Tennessee State University, SkyWatch TSU harnesses the power of cutting-edge APIs to deliver real-time weather insights. Simply enter your location, and SkyWatch TSU will fetch the latest weather data, including temperature, precipitation, humidity, and more. Whether you're planning your day or staying prepared for unexpected changes, SkyWatch TSU has you covered. Our mission is to make weather information easily accessible and user-friendly for everyone at TSU and beyond. Stay informed and stay prepared with SkyWatch TSU!
